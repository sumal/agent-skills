# Human-Like Writing Skill

This skill provides comprehensive instructions on how to generate text that reads naturally and organically, completely avoiding the predictable patterns, rhythmic monotony, and stylistic clichés common in standard AI-generated content.

## The Core Principles: Perplexity & Burstiness

Technical analysis of AI vs. human writing typically focuses on two metrics:

1.  **Perplexity (Predictability of Word Choice):** AI language models tend to pick the most mathematically probable next word, resulting in low perplexity (highly predictable text). Human writing has **high perplexity**—it uses varied, unexpected, and contextually nuanced vocabulary.
2.  **Burstiness (Variation in Structure):** AI writing often has a uniform, steady tempo with consistent sentence lengths (low burstiness). Human writing flows in "bursts"—alternating dramatically between short, punchy sentences and long, winding ones (high burstiness).

---

## Directives for Generation

Whenever you are generating text, articles, emails, or stories under this skill, you MUST strictly adhere to the following rules:

### 1. Maximize Burstiness (Structural Variance)
*   **Sentence Length:** You must significantly vary your sentence lengths. Mix extremely short, impactful sentences (1-6 words) with much longer, complex, and flowing sentences (20+ words). 
*   **Paragraph Structure:** Avoid paragraphs of uniform size. A single-sentence paragraph is powerful. Use it.
*   **Rhythm:** Read your mental draft. If it sounds like a metronome, rewrite it. Break the rhythm.

### 2. Maximize Perplexity (Lexical Diversity)
*   **The "Banned" List:** Absolutely DO NOT use the following overused AI crutch words and phrases under any circumstances:
    *   "Delve into"
    *   "A tapestry of" / "Rich tapestry"
    *   "In today's fast-paced world" / "In the ever-evolving landscape"
    *   "Leverage" (as a verb, unless specifically in a financial context)
    *   "Testament to"
    *   "It's important to remember / note that"
    *   "Ultimately,"
    *   "Furthermore," "Moreover," "In conclusion," "To summarize"
*   **Vocabulary Choice:** Opt for specific, vivid verbs instead of adding adverbs to generic verbs. Use unexpected but contextually accurate adjectives. 
*   **Idioms:** Use idioms, phrasal verbs, and colloquial expressions naturally. 

### 3. Voice, Tone, and Flow
*   **Conversational Tone:** Unless a strictly formal academic tone is requested, write conversationally. Use contractions (e.g., "don't", "can't", "it's").
*   **Imperfect Transitions:** Real human writing doesn't always transition perfectly smoothly from one thought to the next. Allow for slightly abrupt transitions or tangents that circle back to the main point.
*   **Rhetorical Devices:** Use rhetorical questions sparingly to engage the reader, but avoid ending sections with a summarizing question (e.g., "So, what does this mean for the future?").
*   **Opinion and Perspective:** Write with a distinct viewpoint. Generic neutrality is a hallmark of AI. Ground your writing with specific examples rather than broad generalizations.

### 4. The "Human Touch" Final Polish
Before finalizing your output, perform a self-review:
*   *Are there lists perfectly grouped in threes?* Break the pattern.
*   *Does the conclusion sound like a summary paragraph wrapping up a high school essay?* Rewrite it to end on a strong, forward-looking statement or a punchy thought instead.
*   *Is it too polished?* Introduce slight structural imperfections or conversational fillers (e.g., "Well,", "Actually,", "Look,") if the format allows.

## Examples

**❌ AI-Typical (Low Perplexity, Low Burstiness):**
*In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, it is essential for businesses to leverage new technologies to enhance their overall productivity. Furthermore, the integration of artificial intelligence offers a rich tapestry of solutions that can streamline complex workflows. Ultimately, adapting to these changes is a testament to a company's resilience.*

**✅ Human-Like (High Perplexity, High Burstiness):**
*Tech moves fast. Frankly, trying to keep up with every new app or platform is exhausting. But here's the reality: you don't need to adopt everything. You just need to find the specific tool that stops your team from wasting three hours on a Tuesday afternoon. That's the real shift. Not a digital revolution, just getting your time back.*
