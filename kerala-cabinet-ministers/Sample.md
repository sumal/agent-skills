# Nipah Outbreak — Health Minister Decision Brief

**Decision Summary**
Declare Section-2 notification under Kerala Public Health Act 2023 + Epidemic Diseases Act 1897, activate State RRT, lock 3-km containment + 7-km buffer around index case, single-source comms via Health Minister 6 PM daily presser, and route all severe cases to a single designated Nipah hospital (Kozhikode MCH / Ernakulam GH model).

**Why This Works**
- 2018, 2021, 2023, 2024 Nipah responses prove containment + aggressive contact tracing + isolation in one designated facility = case-fatality limited to index cluster.
- Section-2 gives DCs unambiguous authority to seal wards, requisition private hospitals, and mandate testing without fresh Cabinet sanction.
- Single-spokesperson rule prevents the rumour-driven panic that hit GCC/migrant-worker remittance corridors in 2018.

**Options Considered**
- **Option A — Full district lockdown (2018 Kozhikode model):** Maximum suppression; high economic + political cost; justified only if R0>1 confirmed in community.
- **Option B — Targeted micro-containment (3 km / 7 km) + active surveillance (RECOMMENDED):** Proven 2023/2024 model, low collateral damage, scalable.
- **Option C — Surveillance-only, no containment:** Unacceptable — Nipah CFR 40–75%, no licensed vaccine, no specific antiviral.

**Risks**
- Healthcare-worker infection cluster (2018 saw nurse Lini's death) — needs strict PPE + N95 + droplet+contact precautions, no aerosol-generating procedures outside designated isolation.
- Hospital-acquired transmission via shared wards — must vacate non-Nipah patients from index facility within 24h.
- Migrant worker / GCC panic + travel curbs by other states/countries — pre-empt with MEA + Embassies briefing.
- Bat-culling demand from public — resist; ICMR-NIV + KFRI line: do not cull, disrupts ecology and may worsen viral shedding.
- Misinformation in WhatsApp/regional press — counter via Fact Check Kerala + IPRD Malayalam-first bulletins every 6 hours.

**Execution Plan — First 72 Hours**

*Hour 0–6*
- IDSP confirmation → ICMR-NIV Pune sample dispatch (parallel test at Kozhikode/Thonnakkal Virology Institute).
- Section-2 notification signed by Health Minister; gazette within 6 hrs.
- Convene State RRT (DHS, DME, NHM SPM, KMSCL MD, IDSP State Officer, ICMR liaison, NCDC Delhi rep).
- Designate single Nipah treatment facility; evacuate other patients; commission separate isolation block.

*Hour 6–24*
- Contact list freeze: high-risk (P1) and low-risk (P2) categories; route-mapping via call-detail records (Home Dept coordination), CCTV, and OPD registers.
- Containment & buffer zones notified by District Collector under DM Act 2005 §34.
- Cabinet Sub-Committee on Health constituted: CM, Health, Revenue, LSGD, Finance.
- KMSCL: emergency procurement of monoclonal antibody (m102.4) via ICMR + Australia Queensland Health channel; Ribavirin stockpile audit; PPE + N95 + viral transport medium surge to Kozhikode/Ernakulam pattern.
- First Health Minister presser at 6 PM IST — Malayalam first, English follow; single hashtag, one helpline (Disha 1056 + dedicated Nipah cell).

*Hour 24–72*
- Daily 9 AM RRT review + 6 PM Minister presser — non-negotiable cadence.
- Active surveillance: door-to-door fever survey in containment zone via ASHA + Anganwadi + LSGI ward members (Kudumbashree convergence).
- HCW protocol: PPE drills, buddy-checks, daily symptom screen, designated quarantine hostel for off-shift staff.
- School/Anganwadi/public-event closure ONLY in 3-km containment zone (not district-wide).
- Centre coordination: MoHFW, NCDC, ICMR, AIIMS team request, MEA briefing for GCC consulates, FSSAI for fruit-bat zoonotic linkage messaging.
- Bat surveillance via KFRI + ICMR-One Health team — palm-sap (toddy) + half-eaten fruit advisory in vernacular.
- Mental-health + bereavement support cell via NIMHANS-Sahayatra protocol.

**Data Needed (urgent)**
- Confirmed lab status (RT-PCR + sequencing) and whether Bangladesh strain or Malaysia strain.
- Index-case travel/contact map last 21 days.
- Bed + ICU + ventilator count at designated facility; HCW availability roster.
- KMSCL stock: Ribavirin, m102.4, PPE kits (Level 3), N95, viral transport medium.
- GCC / inter-state migrant flow data from index Panchayat (Norka coordination).
- ICMR-NIV BSL-3/4 turnaround time commitment.

**Comms — Single Source of Truth**
- Health Minister: 6 PM daily, Malayalam first, no other Minister speaks publicly on Nipah.
- IPRD bulletins every 6 hrs on official handles only.
- Fact Check Kerala on WhatsApp rumour cycle.
- Specific advisories: avoid raw date-palm sap, wash fruit, no bat-culling, mask + hand hygiene in containment zone, do not visit hospitals unless symptomatic.

**One-Line Political/Federal Posture**
- Stay technical, credit ICMR/NCDC + DCs/LSGIs publicly, avoid Centre-State friction; brief LoP and parliamentary parties before Day 2 to keep messaging unified.
