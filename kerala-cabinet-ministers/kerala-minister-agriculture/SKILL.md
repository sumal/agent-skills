# Kerala Agriculture Minister — Rapid Decision Skill

## Core Mandate & Top 3 KPIs
1. **Food security + paddy revival** — Paddy area + production, Supplyco procurement at MSP+bonus (~₹28.20/kg), wetland conservation under Paddy & Wetland Act 2008.
2. **Plantation-crop economics** — Rubber price stabilisation (RPSS), coconut/copra MSP, cardamom-pepper export competitiveness, climate-resilient varieties via KAU.
3. **Farmer income + dignity** — Karshaka Pension coverage, FPO formation (10,000-FPO national mission), Jaivakeralam organic premium, crop insurance penetration.

## Rapid Decision Matrix (4-step)
1. **Legality** — Kerala Conservation of Paddy Land & Wetland Act 2008, Land Utilisation Order 1967, KLR Act 1963, APMC reforms, Seeds Act, Plant Protection Act, Insecticide Act, Kerala Land Reform Boards.
2. **Exchequer** — Procurement gap (Supplyco corpus + State subsidy), RPSS outflow, Karshaka Pension (~₹600 cr/yr), PMFBY premium share, KIIFB agri-infra pipeline.
3. **Sustainability** — Soil health, declining water table, monsoon erraticism, Western Ghats eco-sensitivity, organic certification integrity, climate-smart agriculture.
4. **LSGI / Welfare** — Krishibhavan-LSGI integration, Kudumbashree JLG farming, SC/ST/Tribal land + crop coverage, women farmers' visibility (~50% of agri labour).

## Top 3 Risks & Crisis SOP

### R1. Crop Calamity (flood, cyclone, wild-elephant raid, blight)
24h: Krishibhavan damage survey + Collector certification; 72h: SDRF norm release, PMFBY claims push, ex-gratia via Cabinet note, RBI relief KCC moratorium request.

### R2. Farmer Protest (procurement delay, low rubber price, paddy land issue)
24h: Director Agri + Supplyco/Rubber Board; 72h: Minister-led tripartite, interim payment via KFC §22, AG opinion if litigation looms.

### R3. Pesticide / Food-Safety / Pesticide-Residue Scandal
24h: Director Agri + FSSAI Kerala; 72h: ban order under Insecticide Act, sample sweep, public advisory in Malayalam, KAU advisory bulletin.

## Kerala-Specific Innovations
1. **Kerala Climate-Smart Cropping Atlas** — KAU + KSCSTE + KSDMA layer of micro-climate + soil + water + price data per village; auto-recommendations to Krishibhavans.
2. **FPO-as-LSGI Convergence** — every block-level FPO co-financed by LSGI Plan + NABARD; Kudumbashree JLG nucleus; integrated with VFPCK markets and KSAMB e-mandi.
3. **Jaivakeralam Premium Stack** — Participatory Guarantee Scheme (PGS-India) + GI tags + diaspora-direct e-commerce (NORKA-linked) for high-margin organic exports (Wayanad, Idukki).
