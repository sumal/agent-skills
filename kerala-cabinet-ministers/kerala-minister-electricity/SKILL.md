# Kerala Electricity Minister — Rapid Decision Skill

## Core Mandate & Top 3 KPIs
1. **Reliable + affordable power** — SAIDI/SAIFI, AT&C losses, tariff differential subsidy contained, rural feeder uptime.
2. **Renewable transition** — RE share in mix (target ≥50% by 2030), rooftop solar capacity, soura subsidy uptake, storage (BESS + pumped) plan.
3. **KSEBL financial health** — Operating margin, regulatory asset clearance, pension liability sustainability (defined-benefit drag).

## Rapid Decision Matrix (4-step)
1. **Legality** — Electricity Act 2003, Tariff Policy 2016/Rules 2024, KSERC Regulations, Kerala Electricity Duty Act, CEA safety code, Renewable Purchase Obligation, Energy Conservation Act 2001.
2. **Exchequer** — Power-purchase cost (~₹15,000+ cr/yr), tariff subsidy outgo, KSEBL capex via KIIFB, central CSS (RDSS, PMSGY-Soura).
3. **Sustainability** — Hydro environmental flow, solar land-use vs paddy/wetland conflict, BESS lifecycle, monsoon dam-safety, climate-stress on hydro.
4. **LSGI / Welfare** — LSGI rooftop solar (Soura LT4), women-headed household priority, SC/ST/tribal hamlet electrification, pump-set tariff for farmers.

## Stakeholder Consultation Map
- **Tier 0:** Chief Secretary, ACS Power, KSEBL CMD/Director(s), Chief Electrical Inspector, ANERT Director, KSERC liaison, AG Kerala
- **Tier 1:** District Panchayats/ULBs (street-light + LSGI solar), KILA
- **Tier 2:** Water Resources Dept (hydro coords), KSDMA (dam ops), e-Mobility Mission, Kochi Metro/Water Metro (large consumers), MSMEs/industries lobby
- **Tier 3 (Centre):** Ministry of Power, MNRE, CEA, POSOCO/NLDC, REC/PFC, NTPC/NHPC (PPAs), CERC
- **Tier 4:** KSEBOA + recognised KSEBL unions, consumers (LT/HT industries, residents), rooftop-solar EPCs, environmental groups

## Top 3 Risks & Crisis SOP

### R1. Reservoir Shutter Coordination / Dam Safety Crisis
24h: KSEBL Generation + Irrigation + KSDMA joint ops; rule-curve based release; downstream alert; 72h: NDSA/CWC brief, independent audit, MoEFCC compliance.

### R2. State-wide Outage / Grid Failure
24h: SLDC + RLDC coordination; restoration sequencing; hospital/water priority; 72h: root-cause CAB inquiry, equipment audit, communication via IPRD.

### R3. KSEBL Strike / Tariff Order Court Challenge
24h: Labour Commissioner + KSEBL CMD; 72h: tripartite at Minister level, AG defence on tariff order, Cabinet note if revenue impact, no ESMA unless essential service truly disrupted.

## Kerala-Specific Innovations
1. **Soura+Storage at LSGI scale** — every panchayat targets 1 MW rooftop + community BESS via ANERT EPC, with KSEBL net-metering and Kudumbashree as last-mile customer-care.
2. **Pumped-Hydro & Floating-Solar at Existing Reservoirs** — convert Idukki/Idamalayar/Banasurasagar headworks into pumped-storage + floating PV, monetising existing land/water without fresh forest impact.
3. **EV-Grid Smart Tariff (TOD-EV)** — KSERC-approved time-of-day EV-only tariff to soak surplus solar at noon and back KSRTC e-buses overnight; tied to PMSGY rooftop targets.
