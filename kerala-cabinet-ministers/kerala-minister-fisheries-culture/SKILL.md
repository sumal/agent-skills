# Kerala Fisheries, Harbour, Cashew & Cultural Affairs Minister — Rapid Decision Skill

## Core Mandate & Top 3 KPIs
1. **Fisher livelihood + safety at sea** — Sea-safety kit coverage, INCOIS PFZ adoption, ex-gratia TAT (₹10 lakh next-of-kin), insurance penetration, fish landing per active boat.
2. **Coastal protection + harbour modernisation** — Sea-wall/groynes/geotube coverage in erosion hotspots, fishing harbours operational, Punargeham coastal resettlement.
3. **Cashew sector revival + Cultural Mission** — KSCDC + Apex Co-op factory utilisation, women cashew-worker welfare, cultural-events cadre dignity, Akademi grants pipeline.

## Rapid Decision Matrix (4-step)
1. **Legality** — KMFR Act 1980 (trawl ban, mesh size), Marine Products Export Act, MS Act, MFRA, Coastal Aquaculture Authority Act 2005, CRZ 2019, Inland Vessels Act, Cashew Workers' Welfare schemes, Copyright Act (cultural).
2. **Exchequer** — Trawl-ban allowance, sea-safety kit subsidy, Punargeham (₹10 lakh/family), KSCDC working capital, Akademi grants.
3. **Sustainability** — Stock assessment (CMFRI), MSY adherence, plastic pollution, sea-erosion + storm-surge climate risk, mangrove restoration, dolphin/turtle bycatch.
4. **LSGI / Welfare** — LSGI in coastal panchayats (Punargeham), Kudumbashree fisher SHGs, women in cashew sector, SC/ST coastal hamlets, traditional fisher community priority.

## Top 3 Risks & Crisis SOP

### R1. Cyclone / Sea-Surge / Boat Disaster (e.g., Ockhi-style)
24h: KSDMA + Coast Guard + Fisheries + Indian Navy SAR; INCOIS bulletins; 72h: ex-gratia, missing-fishermen registry, Punargeham acceleration, NDRF, Cabinet sub-committee, daily Malayalam presser.

### R2. Trawl-Ban / Stock-Crash Dispute (artisan vs mechanised)
24h: Director + union meeting; 72h: tripartite at Minister level, scientific input from CMFRI, Cabinet note on relief, IPRD comms.

### R3. Cashew Factory Closure / Worker Unrest
24h: KSCDC + Apex Co-op + Labour Commissioner; 72h: working-capital infusion, raw-nut import waiver, women-worker alternative livelihoods via Kudumbashree, settlement memo.

## Kerala-Specific Innovations
1. **Smart Sea-Safety Stack** — INCOIS PFZ + GPS+VHF+EPIRB free distribution + Coast Guard handshake + Malayalam alert app, with insurance auto-trigger for missing fishers.
2. **Coastal Resilience Compact (Punargeham 2.0)** — climate-risk-mapped resettlement + fishing-link preservation + livelihood layer (boat-share co-op, value-add via Matsyafed) + LSGI ownership.
3. **Cashew Co-op 4.0 + Cultural Mission Stack** — automated cashew lines retaining women workforce (re-skilled to QC/branding) + GI-tagged Kerala cashew exports via diaspora; cultural Akademi calendar sponsoring touring artists across LSGIs.
