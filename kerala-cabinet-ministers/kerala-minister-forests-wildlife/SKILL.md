# Kerala Forests & Wildlife Minister — Rapid Decision Skill

## Core Mandate & Top 3 KPIs
1. **HWC reduction with rights-based approach** — Death/injury count (people + animals), ex-gratia TAT (₹10 lakh next-of-kin), barrier coverage (rail-fence/elephant-proof trench), early-warning network.
2. **Forest cover + biodiversity** — % geographical area under forest (~29%), ESZ compliance, tiger/elephant census stability, Western Ghats heritage protection.
3. **FRA 2006 implementation + tribal welfare** — Individual + Community Forest Rights titles disposed, tribal resettlement (Aralam/Cheengeri), oorukoottam mainstreamed.

## Rapid Decision Matrix (4-step)
1. **Legality** — Forest (Conservation) Act 1980, Wildlife Protection Act 1972, IFA 1927, FRA 2006, EIA 2006, Biological Diversity Act 2002, KFA 1961, MoEFCC notifications, Western Ghats notification, Kerala Forest Act 1961.
2. **Exchequer** — CAMPA fund, ex-gratia + medical, Project Tiger/Elephant CSS, eco-development committees, KFDC P&L.
3. **Sustainability** — Carbon stock, riparian buffers, climate-resilience corridors, plastic-free sanctuaries, fire-management plan.
4. **LSGI / Welfare** — Gram sabha + oorukoottam consent for FRA, LSGI fence/trench co-funding, Kudumbashree NTFP-based livelihoods, SC/ST forest-fringe families.

## Stakeholder Consultation Map
- **Tier 0:** Chief Secretary, ACS F&W, PCCF/HoFF, CWLW, Director KFRI, AG Kerala
- **Tier 1:** District Collectors, LSGIs in forest fringe, KILA, Tribal Dept (joint)
- **Tier 2:** KFDC, Eco-Dev Committees, Tiger Reserve Directors (Periyar, Parambikulam), KSEB (hydel reservoirs in forest), KSDMA (forest fires)
- **Tier 3 (Centre):** MoEFCC, NTCA, WII, Project Elephant, NCSC/NCST (FRA), MoTA
- **Tier 4:** Tribal apex bodies (oorukoottam federations), farmers in fringe villages, environmental groups, religious sites in forest, recognised forest workers' unions

## Top 3 Risks & Crisis SOP

### R1. Human-Wildlife Conflict Death (elephant trampling, tiger/leopard attack)
24h: DFO + Collector + Police; ex-gratia ₹10 lakh sanction; 72h: animal capture/translocation ToR via CWLW + WII, fence/trench rapid build, Cabinet HWC Sub-Committee, daily comms in Malayalam.

### R2. Forest Fire / Encroachment / Mining-in-Forest Court Action
24h: PCCF rapid response + KSDMA aerial; 72h: HC compliance, encroachment eviction with R&R, FIR/case tracking, MoEFCC report.

### R3. ESZ / Western Ghats / Buffer-zone Public Backlash
24h: AG + CS briefing; 72h: Cabinet note on Kerala-specific representation to MoEFCC, gram-sabha-led ESZ map revision, IPRD strategy, all-party meeting.

## Kerala-Specific Innovations
1. **HWC Real-Time Early-Warning Mesh** — IoT-camera + SMS-to-fringe-village alerts + KSDMA dashboard + KFRI behavioural model; tied to ex-gratia auto-trigger.
2. **FRA Mission Mode + Oorukoottam Stack** — single workflow for IFR/CFR/CR claims with KILA training, gram-sabha digitisation, RoR mutation auto-link.
3. **Eco-Tourism 2.0 — Tribal-Led Carbon-Negative Trails** — Periyar/Parambikulam/Wayanad tribal cooperatives operate low-impact trails priced for premium markets; revenue share into ESZ village development.

Universal MANDATORY format.
