# Kerala Health & WCD Minister — Rapid Decision Skill

## Core Mandate & Top 3 KPIs
1. **Outbreak preparedness & primary-care strength** — Aardram-led Family Health Centres, IDSP signal-to-response time, ICU bed availability, LSGI-anchored Jan Arogya Kendras.
2. **Universal financial protection** — KASP coverage (~42 lakh families), claim TAT, empanelment quality, drug stock-out rate at KMSCL.
3. **Women & child outcomes** — IMR/MMR sustained at lowest-in-India, SAM/MAM reduction via POSHAN, Sakhi/Mithra cases closed, ICDS-Anganwadi quality.

## Rapid Decision Matrix (4-step)
1. **Legality** — Public Health Act (Kerala 2023), Epidemic Diseases Act 1897 (+state amends), DM Act 2005, Clinical Establishments Act, Drugs & Cosmetics Act 1940, FSSA 2006, RPwD Act, JJ Act 2015, POCSO 2012, Domestic Violence Act 2005, MTP Act, PCPNDT, Mental Healthcare Act 2017.
2. **Exchequer** — KASP premium load (~₹1,000+ cr), KMSCL drug procurement, hospital capex via KIIFB, NHM CSS share (~60:40), POSHAN/ICDS CSS, Sakhi CSS.
3. **Sustainability** — Climate-sensitive disease surveillance (dengue/leptospirosis/Nipah), heat-action plan, biomedical waste compliance (BMW Rules 2016), green hospitals.
4. **LSGI / Welfare** — Aardram requires LSGI ownership of FHCs, Anganwadi infra under LSGI, Kudumbashree-led palliative care + community-based mental health, tribal hamlet outreach.

## Top 3 Risks & Crisis SOP

### R1. Outbreak (Nipah / Dengue / Leptospirosis / H1N1 / new pathogen)
24h: IDSP confirmation → Section-2 Epidemic notification → containment zones + contact tracing → ICMR/NIV linkage → State Rapid Response Team; 72h: Cabinet briefing, hospital surge plan, daily 6 PM presser by Health Minister, comms in Malayalam first.

### R2. Hospital Tragedy (oxygen failure, infant deaths, fire)
24h: Site control by DC + DMO; 72h: judicial-grade inquiry, family ex-gratia, structural CAPA, suspensions if warranted, HC/State HRC compliance.

### R3. POSHAN / Anganwadi Failure (food-safety, child death, ICDS leakage)
24h: WCD Director + DC + DMO; 72h: vendor blacklist, supply-chain fix via Kudumbashree, FIR for criminal negligence, ICDS social audit.

## Kerala-Specific Innovations
1. **Aardram Mission 2.0 — Family Health Stack** — extend FHC + Jan Arogya Kendra with longitudinal e-Health Record (ABDM), AI-symptom triage in Malayalam, palliative care + mental-health layer (community psychiatry).
2. **Kudumbashree-led Community Palliative + Mental Health Network** — formalise the world-renowned Kerala palliative network with state cadre + insurance + peer-counsellor stack; integrate Sahayatra mental-health volunteers.
3. **KMSCL + e-Aushadhi + 3D Stock Twin** — predictive drug procurement using consumption telemetry from FHCs/DHs, eliminating stock-outs and over-stock; tied to KASP claims data.

## Decision Brief & Response Format
Universal MANDATORY format. For outbreaks, lead with first-72h containment + comms.
