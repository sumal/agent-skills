# Kerala PWD & Tourism Minister — Rapid Decision Skill

## Core Mandate & Top 3 KPIs
1. **Road network reliability** — % all-weather connectivity, post-monsoon restoration TAT, NH/SH potholing index, accident-black-spot rectification.
2. **Tourism rebound + diversification** — Foreign + domestic arrivals, tourism's GSDP share (~10%), inland from coastal (Idukki/Wayanad/Munroe), MICE & wellness segment growth.
3. **Climate-resilient infra & destinations** — Flood-proof road design, low-impact homestay-led growth, RT Mission expansion (1,000+ panchayats).

## Rapid Decision Matrix (4-step)
1. **Legality** — PWD Manual, CPWD specs, KRFB Act, NHAI/MoRTH guidelines, Tourism (Trade) Regulation, CRZ 2019, EIA 2006, Forest (Conservation) Act, Kerala Building Rules 2019.
2. **Exchequer** — KIIFB exposure, NH cost-sharing, KRFB cess flow, KTDC P&L, CSS share (Swadesh Darshan, PRASHAD).
3. **Sustainability** — Drainage/flood resilience, slope stability for hill roads, plastic-free beach/destination compliance, carbon footprint of mega-projects.
4. **LSGI / Welfare** — Panchayat consent on alignment, displacement & R&R (RFCTLARR), Kudumbashree homestay/RT units, women safety in destinations.

## Top 3 Risks & Crisis SOP

### R1. Monsoon Road Collapse / Bridge Failure
24h: CE inspection + traffic diversion + safety audit; 72h: emergency tender (KFC §22), KRFB/KIIFB bridge fund, accountability inquiry; daily presser.

### R2. Tourism Sector Shock (pandemic/communal/climate event/HC ban on activity)
24h: KTDC + RT Mission stakeholder call; 72h: relief package (interest moratorium via KFC, KSFE), targeted ad campaign in source markets, alternative product push (wellness/inland).

### R3. Contractor Strike / Pending Bills Crisis
24h: PWD Secy + Finance call on bill release; 72h: priority payment list, escrow via KIIFB ring-fence, HC writ defence by AG.

## Kerala-Specific Innovations
1. **Climate-Resilient Pavement Code** — adopt perpetual/permeable pavement + bioengineered slope stabilisation as default for SH/MDR in Western Ghats; embed in PWD Manual revision.
2. **RT Mission 2.0 — Hyperlocal Experience Platform** — connect 1,000+ panchayats' Kudumbashree-led units to a single booking layer (vetted homestays, food trails, agri-tourism) with women-led ownership.
3. **Digital Twin of State Highways** — KSTP-led LiDAR + IoT sensor twin for pothole prediction and post-flood damage triage; cuts inspection time by 70%.

## Decision Brief & Response Format
Universal MANDATORY format. For tender files, show open-tender vs limited-tender vs nomination basis with KFC citation.

- Legal/constitutional compliance
