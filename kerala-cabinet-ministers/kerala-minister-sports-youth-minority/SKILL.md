# Kerala Sports, Youth, Wakf & Minority Welfare Minister — Rapid Decision Skill

## Core Mandate & Top 3 KPIs
1. **Olympic-track athletes + mass sports** — Medal pipeline (Asian/Olympic), district sports academies activation, school sports participation, GV Raja Sports School outcomes.
2. **Youth empowerment + skilling** — KSYWB programmes scale, KKEM-linked youth jobs, drug-free campus drive, youth-led innovation (start-ups via KSEDM/KBIP).
3. **Minority welfare + Wakf reform** — Scholarship TAT, Madrassa modernisation, Wakf property mapping & encroachment removal, recognition of community education infra.

## Rapid Decision Matrix (4-step)
1. **Legality** — Sports Authority of India Act, Wakf Act 1995 + 2013 amend., Minority Educational Institutions Act 2004, Right to Education (minority overlap), JJ Act (juvenile athletes), POCSO (sports coaches), Anti-doping rules (NADA), Kerala Sports Act, Wakf Boards Rules, Minorities Commission Act.
2. **Exchequer** — Stadia capex via KIIFB, athlete monthly stipends, scholarship outgo (multi-source: state + post-matric), Wakf fund integrity.
3. **Sustainability** — Climate-resilient stadia (post-2018), green campuses, youth mental health, sports-injury insurance, sustainable food in sports academies.
4. **LSGI / Welfare** — LSGI-led playgrounds & youth clubs, Kudumbashree-linked youth volunteering, SC/ST/minority/PwD athletes priority, Wakf land for community use.

## Top 3 Risks & Crisis SOP

### R1. Athlete Tragedy / Doping / Sexual-Harassment in Sports Academy
24h: Director + Police + ICC; medical care + family liaison + ex-gratia; 72h: independent inquiry (judicial/SAI), NADA process, suspensions, structural fix (POSH + child-protection officer + counsellor), IPRD comms with privacy.

### R2. Wakf Property Encroachment / High-Profile Litigation
24h: Wakf CEO + AG + DC; 72h: counter-affidavit, GIS mapping push, eviction with legal compliance, Cabinet note if politically sensitive, all-party briefing.

### R3. Minority Scholarship Discontinuation / CSS Pull-back
24h: Minority Welfare Director + Resident Commissioner + Centre liaison; 72h: state-bridge scheme via Cabinet note, scholarship-portal hot-fix, IPRD outreach in Malayalam + community channels.

## Kerala-Specific Innovations
1. **Olympic Mission Kerala** — district academies + GV Raja + private partnerships with KIIFB capex; sports-science layer (Aardram + KAU + MD Univ); minimum stipends for top athletes.
2. **Youth Innovation Stack (KKEM × KSEDM × Yuva Keralam)** — converged youth-skill + start-up + Pravasi-mentor network; integrated with KASE for OJT and diaspora demand.
3. **Wakf Digital Twin + Heritage Layer** — GIS + drone mapping of all Wakf properties + clear-title workflow + minority-education infra plan; integrated with Registration Dept e-stamp + LSGI building permit.
