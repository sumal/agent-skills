# Kerala Transport Minister — Rapid Decision Skill

## Core Mandate & Top 3 KPIs
1. **KSRTC turnaround** — Operating ratio improvement, daily collection, EV fleet share, pension liability containment, scheduled km adherence.
2. **Road safety** — Fatalities reduction (target -50% by 2030 SDG), black-spot rectification, drunk-driving enforcement, helmet/seat-belt compliance.
3. **Clean-mobility transition** — EV adoption (private + commercial), charging-infra density, last-mile feeder network for Kochi Metro / Tvm light metro / Water Metro.

## Rapid Decision Matrix (4-step)
1. **Legality** — MV Act 1988 (+2019 amendments), CMV Rules 1989, KMV Rules, RTC Act 1950, RoSC Act 1985, Carriage by Road Act 2007, Kerala Road Safety Authority Act 2007.
2. **Exchequer** — KSRTC subsidy + KSEB power purchase for EVs, MV tax (~₹4,000+ cr), KIIFB EV-charging pipeline, fuel-cost reimbursement, KSCRTC pension drag.
3. **Sustainability** — Tailpipe emissions, EV-bus lifecycle CO₂, biodiesel/CNG transition, congestion-induced fuel waste.
4. **LSGI / Welfare** — Last-mile feeder via LSGIs, women-only buses (Mahila Sadan), differently-abled accessibility, student concession discipline.

## Stakeholder Consultation Map
- **Tier 0:** Chief Secretary, ACS Transport, KSRTC CMD, Transport Commissioner (MVD), KSCRTC MD, AG Kerala
- **Tier 1:** District RTOs, ULBs/Panchayats (terminals/depots), KILA
- **Tier 2:** KMRL (Kochi Metro), Kochi Water Metro, KRDCL, ANERT (EV), Kerala Road Safety Authority, KSEBL (charging), e-Mobility Mission
- **Tier 3 (Centre):** MoRTH, NHAI, Ministry of Heavy Industries (FAME), NHIDCL, Indian Railways
- **Tier 4:** KSRTC unions (KSTE, etc.), private bus operators, taxi/auto unions, EV OEMs, road-safety NGOs (drivers' associations)

## Top 3 Risks & Crisis SOP

### R1. Mass Casualty Bus Accident / Bridge Collapse
24h: KSRTC/MVD + Police + DC; injured to KASP-empanelled hospitals; ex-gratia; 72h: judicial inquiry, fitness-audit blitz, suspensions, family liaison via DC, IPRD presser.

### R2. Transport Strike (KSRTC, private bus, taxi/auto)
24h: Labour Commissioner ID Act §12 + Transport Secy joint meeting; 72h: tripartite at Minister level, ESMA only if total breakdown, settlement memo.

### R3. Fuel/Power Cost Spike Threatening KSRTC Solvency
24h: CMD + Finance Secy cash-flow review; 72h: Cabinet note on diesel hedge / KSEB tariff for e-buses, fare-revision committee, KIIFB bridge.

## Kerala-Specific Innovations
1. **EV-First Public Transport Stack** — phased KSRTC e-bus + KSEB depot-charging + ANERT solar canopy + KIIFB capex; women-driver corps for women-only services.
2. **SafeKerala 2.0 — AI-Powered Black-Spot & Enforcement** — MVD CCTV + ANPR + AI for over-speeding/no-helmet/no-seatbelt; auto e-challan via VAHAN; black-spot priority with PWD.
3. **Multi-Modal Mobility-as-a-Service (MaaS)** — single app for KSRTC + Kochi Metro + Water Metro + private bus + auto/taxi + Vande Bharat last-mile, with NCMC card and revenue share.
